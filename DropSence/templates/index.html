CSV File Requirements

Flexible Upload! The system intelligently recognizes your column names - you don't need exact matches!

Required Data (Any Column Name)

  • Age - Student age (numeric) - also accepts: "Years", "StudentAge"
  • Gender - Male/Female - also accepts: "Sex", "M/F"
  • Attendance - Percentage (0-100) - also accepts: "AttendanceRate", "Present%"
  • Score - Academic score - also accepts: "Marks", "Grade", "TestScore"
  • Dropout - Yes/No - also accepts: "Status", "Left", "Discontinued"

Auto-Generated (if missing): StudentID, School, Region, Year

Example CSV Format

Age,Gender,Attendance,Score,Dropout
14,Male,85,78,No
15,Female,92,85,No
16,Male,45,52,Yes
13,Female,88,82,No

This minimal format works! System auto-generates missing fields.
