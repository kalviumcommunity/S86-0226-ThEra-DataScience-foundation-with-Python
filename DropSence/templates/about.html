About DropSence

Empowering NGOs with Data-Driven Education Insights

Our Mission

DropSence is an advanced analytical platform built specifically for Non-Governmental Organizations (NGOs) and educational institutions. Our mission is to leverage data science to identify, predict, and prevent student dropouts before they happen.

Why DropSence?

By utilizing predictive modeling and comprehensive statistical analysis, DropSence removes the guesswork from educational interventions. Our dynamic dashboards allow administrators to rapidly identify "At-Risk" students based on their attendance and academic scores, enabling timely, targeted actions to keep students in the classroom.
